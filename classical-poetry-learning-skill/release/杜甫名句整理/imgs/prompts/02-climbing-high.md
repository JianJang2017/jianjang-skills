# 场景图 Prompt - 登高

## 用途
文章后半段场景配图，展现杜甫晚年登高远眺的苍凉意境。

## 画面描述
An elderly Tang dynasty poet standing alone at the top of a high cliff or terrace in late autumn, looking out over a vast landscape. Below, the mighty Yangtze River rolls endlessly eastward with powerful currents. Around him, bare trees with falling leaves, migrating birds flying in formation across a grey autumn sky. The poet wears a thin worn coat, his hair and beard windblown, holding onto the railing. The composition emphasizes the vastness of nature against the smallness of the solitary human figure. Dramatic atmospheric perspective with layered mountains fading into mist.

## 风格
Traditional Chinese landscape painting, inspired by Du Fu's "Climbing High" (登高). Grand, melancholic, and contemplative atmosphere.

## 技术参数
- 用途：文章内配图
- 比例：16:9
- 目标尺寸：1080×608
- 禁止：任何文字、印章、水印
