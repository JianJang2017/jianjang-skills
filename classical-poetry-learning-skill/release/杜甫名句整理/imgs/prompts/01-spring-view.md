# 意境图 Prompt - 春望

## 用途
文章核心意境配图，展现杜甫忧国忧民的情怀。

## 画面描述
A desolate scene of a ruined ancient Chinese capital city in spring. Broken walls and collapsed palace gates overgrown with wild grass and weeds. In the foreground, flowers bloom sadly among the ruins, and birds fly across the grey sky. A lone figure of a middle-aged Tang dynasty poet in tattered robes stands amid the devastation, looking up at the sky with tears in his eyes. The contrast between natural beauty (spring flowers, green grass) and human destruction (ruined buildings) is emphasized. Muted colors with touches of green and red flowers against grey ruins.

## 风格
Traditional Chinese painting style, inspired by Du Fu's "Spring View" (春望). Melancholic and powerful atmosphere.
