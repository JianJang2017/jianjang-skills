# 场景图 Prompt

## 用途
文章中后段的场景配图，展现李白晚年遇赦东归的喜悦。

## 画面描述
A small wooden boat sailing swiftly through the dramatic Three Gorges of the Yangtze River. Towering cliff walls on both sides, with morning clouds and mist swirling around the peaks. The boat carries a single passenger - an elderly Tang dynasty poet in white robes, standing at the bow with a joyful expression, his robes and hair blowing in the wind. Gibbons calling from the treetops on the cliffs. Golden morning sunlight illuminating the scene from behind the mountains. Dynamic composition showing the speed of the boat.

## 风格
Traditional Chinese landscape painting, inspired by Li Bai's "Leaving Baidi Castle at Dawn". Joyful and liberated atmosphere.

## 技术参数
- 用途：文章内配图
- 比例：16:9
- 目标尺寸：1080×608
- 禁止：任何文字、印章、水印
