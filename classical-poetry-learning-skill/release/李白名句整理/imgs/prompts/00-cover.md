# 封面底图 Prompt

## 用途
李白名句整理公众号文章封面底图（无文字），文字将由 compose-cover.js 合成。

## 画面描述
A serene traditional Chinese ink wash landscape painting. Misty mountains in the background with a lone figure of a Tang dynasty poet standing by a river, gazing at the full moon reflected in the water. Pine trees on a cliff edge. Soft golden moonlight illuminating the scene. Ethereal clouds drifting between mountain peaks. Color palette: ink black, warm gold, misty white, subtle blue-green tones. Traditional Chinese painting style with watercolor washes. No text, no characters, no seals.

## 风格
Traditional Chinese ink wash painting (水墨画), elegant and poetic atmosphere.

## 技术参数
- 用途：微信公众号封面底图（后续合成文字）
- 比例：2.35:1
- 目标尺寸：900×383
- 禁止：任何文字、印章、水印
