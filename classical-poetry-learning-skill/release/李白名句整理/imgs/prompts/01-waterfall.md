# 意境图 Prompt

## 用途
文章开头的意境配图，展现李白诗歌的豪迈气质。

## 画面描述
A magnificent waterfall cascading down from an extremely tall mountain cliff, surrounded by lush green vegetation and mist. The water crashes down with tremendous force, creating a rainbow in the spray. A tiny figure of a Tang dynasty poet in white robes stands at the bottom, looking up in awe at the spectacular view. Dramatic lighting with sunbeams breaking through clouds. Traditional Chinese landscape painting style with vivid colors.

## 风格
Traditional Chinese landscape painting, inspired by Li Bai's "View of the Waterfall at Mount Lu". Grand and awe-inspiring atmosphere.

## 技术参数
- 用途：文章内配图
- 比例：16:9
- 目标尺寸：1080×608
- 禁止：任何文字、印章、水印
